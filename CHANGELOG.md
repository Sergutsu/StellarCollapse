### Added

- **P10 — understandability & navigation.** The game finally explains itself and can't be mis-clicked into a corner:
  - **HOW TO PLAY manual.** A paged in-game field manual (THE LOOP · DISPATCHING · THE MINIGAMES · THE STATION · CONTROLS & HOTKEYS) opened by the new top-bar `? HELP` button, the `H` / `?` hotkey, or the pause menu's HOW TO SHIFT. On **first boot** it opens over the MISSION BOARD with a `START SHIFT` CTA; the `ui.helpSeen` flag (an additive save field) stops it from re-opening. Copy lives in the pure, tested `src/help-content.js`.
  - **Pause menu replaces instant exit.** `ESC` (or the in-run `PAUSE` button on both puzzle and combat scenes) freezes the shift and offers RESUME / HOW TO SHIFT / ABORT SHIFT. Combat shifts — which previously had no way out at all on touch — get a PAUSE button too. **Aborting now settles as a failed run** (reduced rep, +6 hull wear), matching the idle RETURN rule, instead of paying full win-rate rep for ten seconds of play.
  - **Global hotkeys.** `ESC` close/pause · `H`/`?` manual · `1`–`6` hub tabs · `M` MISSION BOARD · `P` pause. Wired through the new context-aware `src/hotkeys.js`.
  - **RESET asks first.** Wiping the profile takes a confirmation dialog now — one stray click can no longer delete a save.
  - **Results screen says what to do next.** Context-aware NEXT hints (pure `src/results-hints.js`): hull damage → SHIPYARD, ore → MARKET refinery, a found warp cell → STAR MAP, a crew level-up → CREW.
- **The MISSION BOARD is reachable again (and is the boot surface).** `_openMissionBoard()` had zero call sites — the pillar-2 "click a card" deploy surface was built and never shown. The board now opens at boot, after every settled shift, via the planner's MISSION BOARD button, or the `M` hotkey; closing it by hand is respected across tab flips. The core loop (BOARD → SHIFT → REPORT → BOARD) is closed.

With no free assets the shift still launches — flown solo at base rate, and the news ticker says so.

### Removed

- **The dead settings gear** in the hub top bar (no handler since P2). Cut in favor of `? HELP`.

- **P9 — the hub's left column is now contextual per tab.** STAR MAP, BUILD/UPGRADE and MARKET can each take over the bay that used to hold only ACTIVE MISSIONS / ACTIVE RESEARCH: a tab opts in with `usesSidePanel` + `sidePanelTitle` and implements `layoutSide({width,height})`, building into the hub-provided `side.list`. The hub still owns the frame, so ADR-0010 holds. See [`docs/UI-HUB.md`](docs/UI-HUB.md) §3a.
- **STAR MAP reads as a star system.** Planets are shaded spheres with type-specific surfaces (Ocean / Terrestrial / Desert / Gas Giant / Ice Giant: atmosphere halo, latitude bands, continents, ice caps, ring systems for the giants, limb light) and a night side rotated toward the central star; moons are cratered discs. Classification sub-labels, a selection reticle and a highlighted orbit ring make the picked body obvious, and the backdrop speckle was thinned so the system — not the sky — is the subject.
- **SYSTEM DATA board in the left panel**, with a `SYSTEM INDEX` of every body in the system (tap to select). It replaces the floating panel that used to swim around the map chasing the body it described.
- **MARKET price chart.** The center panel is a TradingView-style chart for the selected good: grid, area + line series, price/time axes, `H / L / BASE`, 24 h and vs-close change, a last-price tag, amber markers at each UTC-midnight re-price, and a pointer crosshair that snaps to the nearest hour. The goods list moved to the left panel and doubles as the watchlist.
- **The hub's view code is now executed in CI.** `tests/hub-scene-smoke.test.js` boots the real `HubScene` + tabs against a headless Pixi stand-in (`tests/helpers/pixi-mock.js`, mapped by a Node resolve hook so no dependency is installed) and drives the lifecycle: activate all six tabs, resize 1920×1080 → 820×560, tap every row and button, dispatch, tear down — asserting no throws, no non-finite geometry, one visible owner of the shared left bay, and profile changes that match what the UI quoted. It skips itself on Node < 20.6. It found all three bugs below.
- **`economy.priceHistory()`** — a deterministic, derived price series (24 hourly points, ±6% hashed intraday wobble) whose right edge is exactly today's `marketPrices()` mid, so the chart can never disagree with the order ticket. Nothing is stored in the save.
- **P8 — the meta loop is complete.** Six new pure modules (`reputation`, `daily`, `economy`, `star-map`, `crew`, `settlement`) give the station real rules, and every reward now flows through one settlement applied in one atomic save. See [`docs/META-SYSTEMS.md`](docs/META-SYSTEMS.md) and [ADR-0011](docs/adr/0011-meta-economy-single-source.md).
- **Reputation ranks.** Six tiers (Apprentice → Master Dispatcher) earned by finishing dispatches. A new REP chip in the top bar shows rank + progress; T8/T9 contracts are REP-gated and say which clearance they need instead of launching.
- **Daily contract board.** The board rolls from a per-UTC-day seed, so it is stable across reloads and refreshes free at midnight. Paid rerolls escalate 150 → 300 → 450 CR (6/day, +1 with Countermeasures research), and the modal shows the price, rolls used and the countdown to the free refresh.
- **Working market.** Seven goods (six ores + minerals) with daily price drift, a 28% buy/sell spread that Trade Compact and a charted Nova Bazaar bend in your favour, quoted BUY/SELL lots of 10 or 50, and a market-news headline naming the day's movers.
- **Refinery — minerals finally have a faucet.** Melt ore into minerals 4:1 (common) or 2:1 (rare), which is what feeds the shipyard and research sinks.
- **Sector network.** Thirteen chartable sectors on the STAR MAP rail with warp costs, threat ratings and permanent bonuses; PLOT COURSE (was a stub) now spends warp, banks the discovery grant and charts the sector. Warp cells are *found* — exploration, salvage and combat dispatches bring one home.
- **Crew progression.** Members earn XP per dispatch (more when their role fits the contract), level to 20, and their level multiplies the contract payout. Roster is capped by berths (Habitat Extension adds one), hiring costs escalate past five heads, and dismissals pay 30% severance.
- **Hull wear + repair.** Dispatches damage the hull they flew (risk-scaled, worse on a loss, softened by plating/shields/Driftyard 9); the shipyard repairs at 3 minerals per point.
- **Combat runs pay resources.** `DefenseLedger` maps destroyed formations, power-ups and the boss onto the six-ore identity, so a Combat contract banks ore and REP instead of credits only, and the MISSION REPORT relabels its rows (WAVE / BALLS LOST / PIXELS / WRECKS / PICKUPS).
- **WELCOME BACK banner.** Boot computes what finished while you were away and offers CLAIM ALL; a 30 s heartbeat plus `pagehide`/`visibilitychange` stamping keeps the offline window honest.
- **MISSION REPORT settlement lines.** REP (+ promotion call-out), crew XP and level-ups, hull wear, warp found, charted sector, and an itemised credit breakdown.
- **ACTIVE BONUSES card** on the RESEARCH tab listing exactly what the completed nodes do — every effect key now has a consumer.
- **Save v2** with in-place migration from v1 (banked REP, crew XP, charted sectors, board state, lifetime stats); the storage key is unchanged and a malformed or future blob still falls back to a starter profile.

### Fixed

- **Idle dispatch was broken for everybody.** `hub-scene.js` called `buildIdleMissions(...)` when sending a contract out autonomously but never imported it, so every IDLE dispatch threw a `ReferenceError` and silently did nothing (manual dispatch skipped that branch, which is why it looked intermittent). The import is in place, and `tests/module-imports.test.js` now fails the build if any file calls a sibling module's export it did not import.
- **The STAR MAP moved too fast to read.** Orbital motion now runs at `ORBIT_TIME_SCALE = 0.25`: on the seeded system planets take 64 s – 3.4 min per orbit on screen instead of 16 – 51 s, and moons 17 – 36 s instead of 4 – 9 s. The moon/ship reveal thresholds dropped to match (1.0 / 0.85 × fit-zoom), and the new `Orbit:` read-out on the SYSTEM DATA board reports the period the player actually watches.
- **A fresh station had one fleet berth.** `effects.fleetSlots` counts *extra* berths and starts at 0, but the shipyard read it as the capacity — so a starter profile showed `4/1` berths and refused every BUILD order ("All 1 berths occupied"). Berths are now `crew.fleetSlotLimit(effects)` = 10 base + research extras, exposed as `MetaState.fleetSlots()` alongside `crewSlots()`, and the mother-ship card spells the sum out (`Base 10 berths + 2 from research = 12`).
- **The three left-panel owners fought over the shared bay.** STAR MAP rebuilt its board by clearing `side.list` itself, which destroyed the containers the SHIPYARD and MARKET tabs had parked there — the yard's BUILD buttons came back dead after one visit to the star map. Each tab now keeps its content in its own container and clears only that, hides it on `hide()`, and the hub hides every non-owner's container when the bay changes hands.
- **The ship-fit bonus was dead code.** The modifier tested `className.toLowerCase().includes(missionType)`, which can never match a starter hull name, so the ±12%/−6% fit math never fired. `crew.SHIP_CLASS_AFFINITY` now maps hull classes to mission types properly — and a dispatch with no assigned hull stays neutral instead of being penalised.
- **Research multipliers could square themselves.** `resolveEffects(['x','x'])` applied a node's modifier twice; ids are deduped before merging.
- **Panel accents silently fell back to cyan.** `redrawHologramPanel` was handed the `{ accent }` object its callers already pass to `drawHologramPanel`; it now accepts either form.
- **Market sells were no-ops.** `_rawAddOre` clamped the *delta* at zero, so selling ore (a negative delta) did nothing; only the resulting balance clamps now.

- **Missions could brick themselves.** A manually-dispatched run permanently consumed its ship + crew ("On Mission" forever); after a reload they came back only as slow "recovery placeholder" jobs, eventually leaving zero free assets so DISPATCH stayed greyed out. Manual runs now free their ship + crew the moment you press CONTINUE on the results screen (`completeManualMission`), so the next DISPATCH is always available.

### Fixed

- **Idle mission recovery (PR #64).** Ship and crew availability now correctly resets to `On Station` / `Available` when an idle mission completes or is aborted, preventing permanently-deployed ghost assets.
- **Game UI leak on boot (PR #54).** The puzzle HUD no longer flashes behind the missions screen when the hub first loads.
- **Scaled click mapping (PR #55).** Click-to-match coordinates now translate correctly through the scene-scale transform so matches fire on the right cell on mobile and scaled desktops.
- **Idle mission UI node leak (PR #61).** Detached idle-fleet rows are now destroyed instead of merely removed, preventing a slow memory leak on long sessions.
- **PR #52 follow-up — STAR MAP (and any future tab scene) now lays out correctly on first click.** When the user clicked an extracted tab for the first time, `_setActiveTab` called `scene.show()` (which lazy-builds the scene's Pixi nodes), but the `_layoutCenterPanel` fan-out that normally positions tab content only ran at hub-build time — before the nodes existed and before `scene.layout()` could do anything. Result: the coordinate grid rendered empty, all 8 sector pins stacked at (0, 0), and the legend + thumbnail cards overlapped at the panel origin until a window resize kicked off a fresh layout pass. Fix: `HubScene._layoutCenterPanel` now stashes the panel's inner `width / height` on the center object, and `_setActiveTab` calls `scene.layout({ width, height })` immediately after `scene.show()` whenever those dims are known. Covers STAR MAP today and RESEARCH in this same PR.
- **PR #52 follow-up — `StarMapTab` no longer leaks Text objects on every resize.** `_layout()` rebuilt the ~12-13 longitude/latitude tick labels on every invocation by calling `n.axisLabels.removeChildren()` before re-adding new `Text` children — but `removeChildren` only detaches, it does not destroy, so the orphaned `Text` objects (and their GPU textures + style data) accumulated across resize events. Replaced the detach loop with an explicit `removeChild + destroy({ children: true })` drain loop. No visible change; long sessions just stop slowly leaking.

## [0.1.0] — 2025-04-18 (P0 complete)

First "playable prototype" milestone. The game is Pixi-rendered on one URL (`https://sergutsu.github.io/StellarCollapse/`), has 9 tiers, 9 field sizes, a mission-select start screen, and a persistent leaderboard. No idle, no persistent credits, no upgrade tree yet.

### Added

- **Mission-select start screen** (PR #32). 3×3 grid of mission cards, one per ranked tier. Click a card to deploy — no mode/complexity/size toggles, no BEGIN button. "CHIEF DISPATCHER" identity card with per-session callsign replaces the player-name input.
- **6-ore catalogue** (PR #32). Red→Pyrite, blue→Cryonite, green→Verdanite, yellow→Helium, bomb→Volatiles, snake→Biomass. Mission cards preview expected ores; Collapsed missions preview the two rare ores.
- **Seeded mission roll** (PR #32). Asteroid names are stable within a session and vary across page loads, via a deterministic Mulberry32 RNG seeded per boot.
- **Pixi starfield + procedural nebulae** (PR #19–#23). Blinking dot stars + cross-sparkle stars + baked-once nebula `RenderTexture`. Parallax drift.
- **Scanner sweep** (PR #20, #21). Transparent board over space; diagonal scanner band reveals the grid as it passes.
- **Pixi HUD port** (PR #17, #18). Score / level / tips / controls / previews / title bar all render on the Pixi stage.
- **Pixi board port** (PR #15, #16). Gradient cells, emoji icons, particle bursts, pulse tickers, snake trail pool.
- **9 ranked tiers** (PR #12). All mode × complexity combos get a leaderboard.
- **Level-speed ramp** (PR #12). Level-1 drop 1000 ms, step −100 ms, floor 200 ms. Level-up every 8 lines.
- **Field-size multipliers** (PR #13). Small ×1.5, medium ×1, large ×0.75. Visible on mission cards indirectly via the scoring formula.
- **Low-FX mode** (PR #13). Past 240 cells, expensive per-cell animations are skipped. Pixi renders all sizes at 60 fps.
- **Special arming timer** (PR #11). In Collapsed, bomb/snake cells have a 5 s arming clock; if unused, they morph into a random normal-colour tile.
- **In-game HUD rework** (PR #8, #9). Level panel left, score/lines right, title bar with reactive star, per-level tips, controls panel, smaller board glow.
- **Field-size selector + 3 sizes per complexity** (PR #7). No `10×20`.
- **Gameplay modes + complexities + 6-tier leaderboard** (PR #3).
- **GitHub Pages deploy + CI tests workflow** (PR #4).

### Changed

- **"Classic" → "Stellar"** for the match-4 mode (PR #6). Legacy `classic-*` tier scores auto-migrate to `stellar-*`.
